Step by step

How to connect the PS5 controller to the Nintendo Switch

Scroll the page and follow the animation. The screens shown on the console are the app's own.

Loading the 3D model
1

Put the app on the SD card

First, make a copy of the atmosphere/contents/010000000000bd00 folder on the card, to be safe. Then download the app and unzip it.

  1. In the CNX pack, hold R and open the Album to enter the Homebrew menu.
  2. Open DBI, start the MTP connection and plug the USB cable into the computer.
  3. On the computer, open Switch, then 1: SD Card and the switch folder.
  4. Copy the unzipped Control PS-5 folder there.

Prefer no cable? Put the SD card in the computer, copy the folder into switch and put the card back in the Switch.

2

Open the app and install the driver

Leave DBI and go back to the Homebrew menu (R + Album). Open Control PS-5 and, on the Home screen, tap the purple Install driver button. When it finishes, the app asks you to restart the Switch.

3

Tap Pair controller

With the Switch back on, open Control PS-5 again. Tap Pair controller and, on the Controllers screen that opens, choose Change Grip/Order.

4

Hold Create and PS

On the PS5 controller, hold Create and PS until the light bars start blinking. That is how the DualSense enters pairing mode.

5

Press L1 and R1 together

On the Press L + R screen, press L1 and R1 together. When it syncs, a black controller appears on screen and the light bars stay on, without blinking.

6

Done: set it up your way

Go back to the app and adjust triggers, lights and profiles. The controller shows as DualSense, Player 1 and the driver as Active.

Features

What Control PS-5 unlocks on the DualSense

Everything is set up inside the Switch, without moving folders or editing files.

Adaptive triggers on L2 and R2

Resistance, gun click, bow, vibration and machine gun. Each trigger has ready-made effects and fine-tuning of start, end and strength.

Light bar and LEDs

Twelve quick colors, fine-tuning, brightness, mute button LED and player LEDs. The preview shows on the controller right away.

Automatic per-game profile

Set it up once. When the game opens, its profile is applied by itself; the others use the default.

One-tap installation

The app installs, updates and uninstalls the driver, and keeps a backup of the MissionControl you already had.

Guided pairing

Shows the steps on a drawing of the controller and opens the Switch Controllers screen so you can pair another one.

Controller test

Buttons, sticks, gyroscope, battery and touchpad, live. You can even test PS and mute: on the Test tab they do not take you off the screen.

Touchpad as cursor, for a broken touchscreen

Your finger moves a little hand with a purple glow and the click taps where it is, even on the HOME menu. A lifesaver if your Switch touchscreen is broken. It also works as a direct touchscreen.

What you need to know

Compatibility

Switch 1 hacked with Atmosphère (CNX pack). Requires firmware 23.0.1 and Atmosphère 1.12.0, the versions the driver was built and tested on. Works with the DualSense and DualSense Edge.

Limits

The game sound does not come out of the controller speaker, because the Switch does not send audio to controllers. Trigger effects are set per profile: the game does not know they exist.

New firmware and undoing

The app checks the firmware before installing and refuses when it is newer than supported, so the boot does not hang. To go back, tap Uninstall: it restores the original MissionControl.

Use at your own risk. Modifying the console may void the factory warranty, and using a modified console online carries a ban risk. Keep a backup of the SD card. The app does not enable piracy.
